Delete Your Account

Available without the app installed

You can permanently delete your Blur account from this page. Your profile is hidden right away, and deletion can't be undone.

What happens right away

  • Your profile disappears from Discover, matches, and Auto Match right away.
  • Your photos stop being shown to anyone. They're erased with the rest of your data (below).
  • You're signed out everywhere — every device and every session. Your sign-in stops working at once, or shortly after if our sign-in service is briefly unreachable.
  • Pending requests end — any reveal requests, vibe replies, or active matches involving you are cancelled.
  • Your conversations are removed for both people — from your chats and from the chats of everyone you talked to.
  • Your name is removed from other members' notifications in Blur.

What happens next

We'll then erase your profile, photos, messages, matches, verification material and the rest of your Blur data from our active systems. This happens on our side; you don't need to sign in again.

You can create a new account later. Your old profile, photos, chats and matches don't carry over — though some safety protections can (see below).

Blur doesn't sell subscriptions or purchases in the app. If you bought anything through Google Play or the App Store, deleting your account doesn't cancel it — manage it in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, or on iPhone in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

What we keep, and why

  • A minimal record that the account was deleted (no name or email), so the deletion stays applied even if a backup is ever restored.
  • A record that you accepted our terms, and security logs of sign-ins and account changes, for a limited time.
  • If there's a safety report about you, erasing your data waits until a decision has been made on it. Safety reports that involve you, and moderators' decisions about them, stay with those cases — including any copies of messages or files kept as part of a report.
  • If another member blocked you, or your account was restricted or reported, we keep a protected record of your verified sign-in details for a limited time, so those safety protections can still apply if you sign up again.
  • For a limited time, the people you talked to see a placeholder where your conversation was — without your name or photo — so they can still report it.
  • Copies in backups aren't erased instantly. If a backup is ever restored, your deletion is applied again.
  • Copies outside Blur — screenshots, saved photos, or push notifications and emails already delivered — can't be recalled.

Questions about your data: privacy@blurmeet.com. See our Privacy Policy for the full data-handling policy.

Delete specific data without deleting your account

Some types of data can be removed without closing your account. You can delete individual profile photos at any time from within the Blur app — this removes them from our storage, not just from view. If you want to request deletion of other specific data while keeping your account, email privacy@blurmeet.com and tell us what you'd like removed.

Prefer not to delete?

If you only need a break, you can pause your account in the app instead: you're hidden, your chats are on hold, and nothing is deleted. Deletion is meant for when you're certain.
