Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Blur is a relationship app where people connect through conversation before photos are revealed. This policy explains what information we collect to run the app, how we use it, and the real choices you have — including how to delete your account or specific pieces of your data.

1. Privacy at Blur

This policy applies to the Blur mobile app and its supporting services. It describes our actual, current data practices — not a future roadmap. If something changes about how Blur handles your data, we'll update this page and adjust the date above.

2. Information We Collect

To create and run your account, we collect:

  • Name and date of birth — your display name and birth date, used to show your profile and confirm you meet our age requirement.
  • Email and phone number — used to create your account, sign you in, and verify you're a real person via a one-time code sent by SMS.
  • Gender and who you're looking to meet — used to power matching and discovery.
  • Profile photos — required to complete a profile; shown to other users.
  • Bio and prompt answers — text (and optionally a short voice recording) you write or record to answer profile prompts; shown to other users.
  • Identity verification material — an optional selfie photo or short video, and for Aadhaar-based verification, information passed through a dedicated verification provider. Used only to confirm you're a real, verified person.
  • Location — your approximate location, used to show you people nearby. You can decline this and enter your city manually instead — Blur works either way.
  • Messages — the content of conversations you have with matches, so the chat feature works.
  • Device and diagnostic information — a push-notification token (if you allow notifications), crash and error reports, and general information about how you use the app (which features you open, not a behavioral profile).

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to operate Blur's core features: creating and securing your account, showing your profile to others, powering discovery and matching, enabling chat, verifying you're a real and eligible person, and diagnosing bugs or crashes so we can fix them.

We do not use your activity in the app to build a behavioral profile or feed an algorithmic recommendation engine. Blur's matching is intentionally simple — it does not try to learn and predict who you'll like based on your usage patterns.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data, and Blur has no advertising or marketing SDKs of any kind — there is nothing in the app set up to share your data with ad networks.

We do share limited data with a small number of service providers, only as needed to run the features above:

  • Phone number is sent to our SMS provider solely to deliver your one-time verification code.
  • Crash and error data is sent to our error-monitoring provider to help us diagnose and fix bugs. Authentication tokens and cookies are stripped from this data before it's sent.
  • Aadhaar-based verification is processed through a dedicated third-party identity-verification provider, used only to confirm that specific verification step.
  • Photos and other files are stored with our cloud infrastructure provider, used only to host them on our behalf.

These providers process your data only to help us provide the service — not for their own separate purposes.

5. Photos and Media

Profile photos are required to complete a Blur profile and are shown to other users. Identity-verification images and any verification video are not shown to other users — they're reviewed internally by our safety team to confirm your identity, and stored separately from your public profile.

You can delete an individual photo at any time from within the app without deleting your account — this removes it from our storage as well, not just from view.

6. Messages and Communications

Chat is optional — you can use Blur without ever sending a message. Message content is stored so the conversation feature works and so both participants can see their own chat history. If you delete your account, your existing conversations remain visible to whoever you were speaking with, but no further messages can be sent by either side.

7. Verification and Safety

Identity verification (a selfie or short video, or Aadhaar-based verification) is optional and not required to use Blur's core features, but it affects the trust signals shown on your profile. Selfie/video verification is reviewed by our internal safety team; Aadhaar-based verification runs through a dedicated verification provider as a two-step process. We also maintain reporting and blocking tools so users can flag or avoid people who violate our terms.

8. Device and Technical Information

If you allow notifications, we store a device token to deliver them — you can decline this permission and still use Blur. We always collect crash and error diagnostics and general app-interaction data (such as which screens or features are used) to keep the app working and fix problems; this isn't something you can turn off individually, and it isn't used to build a personalized recommendation profile.

9. Storage and Service Providers

Blur runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS), which hosts our servers and stores photos and other files you upload. We use a dedicated SMS provider to deliver verification codes, an error-monitoring provider for crash diagnostics, and a dedicated identity-verification provider for Aadhaar-based checks. Each of these providers only processes the specific data needed for its purpose.

10. Data Retention

When you delete your account, most of your personal data is removed immediately — see Account Deletion below for exactly what happens right away.

Some information — your account record, message history, and identity verification material — may be retained for a period afterward. We keep this only for specific, legitimate reasons: to resolve safety or fraud reports that were already in progress, to avoid orphaning other users' own records (like a report they filed, or a match you shared), and to meet legal obligations.

We don't currently have an automated, fixed-schedule process that purges this retained data on a set timeline. If you'd like retained data removed sooner, email privacy@blurapp.in and tell us what you'd like deleted — we'll act on it directly.

11. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and its data at blurmeet.com/delete-account, or from within the app. Deletion takes effect immediately:

  • Your profile stops appearing in Discover, matches, and search right away.
  • Your photos are deleted from our storage.
  • You're signed out on every device, and your login stops working.
  • Any pending reveal requests, vibe replies, or active matches involving you are cancelled.
  • Your existing conversations become permanently read-only for whoever you were speaking with.

This cannot be reversed. See Data Retention above for what happens to the small amount of information kept afterward.

12. Requests to Delete Specific Data

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove some of your data. You can delete individual profile photos at any time from within the app — this permanently removes them from our storage. For other specific data you'd like removed while keeping your account, email privacy@blurapp.in and tell us what you'd like deleted; we'll handle it directly rather than through an automated self-service tool.

13. Security

Data sent between the Blur app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Access to our internal systems is limited to people who need it to operate the service. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we take reasonable, ongoing steps to protect your information and will notify you if we become aware of an incident affecting your data.

14. Children's Privacy / Age Requirements

Blur is intended only for people 18 years of age or older. We check your date of birth when you create an account and do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to register. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will remove it.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as Blur changes. If we make a change that materially affects how we handle your data, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

16. Contact Us

For privacy questions or to request that specific data be deleted, email privacy@blurapp.in. For general support, email support@blurapp.in.
